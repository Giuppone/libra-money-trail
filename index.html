What the page lets you do

Pick any of the 23 exit points. You see all the paths that reach it, so you can see how the money was split and joined again, with one main route in orange. Each step of that route has a date, an amount and a link to a public blockchain explorer, so you do not have to trust me. You can check it yourself.

The dashboard also shows nine known patterns, such as liquidity added on one side only and fragmented withdrawals. It works best on a large screen.

What this work does not show

  • It does not claim that a crime was committed, and it does not identify people. Only the courts can ask a service who a customer is.
  • An address on the list does not mean its owner did anything wrong. Many are exchange wallets with millions of customers.
  • Wallets are called "team" only when a public source says so. Exchange names come from public labels that the exchanges did not confirm.
  • The trail stops at exchanges and services, because the money mixes there with the money of other customers.
  • We analyzed about 957,000 transactions. This site shows the ones behind the findings, not all of them.
